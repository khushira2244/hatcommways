# Human-led governance

[Documentation index](README.md)

Governance is an authority boundary. Configuration and assessment surface risks, missing approvals, policy concerns and decision context. Strands reasoning is advisory; people and organizations retain authority.

Assessment does not establish legal compliance, grant external permissions or autonomously govern communities. Human approval and deterministic lifecycle/version checks remain necessary where required. Broader delegation and governance expansion in older design documents are future directions, not a deployed feature inventory.
