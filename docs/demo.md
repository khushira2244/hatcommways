# Judge demo walkthrough

[Documentation index](README.md)

Use separate organizer, actor and sponsor sessions. Keep credentials private. Allow async execution time and refresh results instead of resubmitting duplicates.

1. **Discover:** Search a real event by name/location and open Event Home.
2. **Plan:** Show goal, governance assessment, stages, work, dependencies and actor requirements; explain human proposal review.
3. **Participate:** Another account chooses work/role and availability, reviews guidance and submits. Show pending participation and organizer approval.
4. **Execute:** Show Actor Dashboard responsibilities. Report a change against an actual work window; lateness needs concrete impact to become a blocker.
5. **Respond:** Interpret, assess, resolve affected work and coordinate. If sufficient, show WORKING/CLEARED. Otherwise show selective replanning, human decision, preserved unrelated work and updated actor state. Do not force replanning.
6. **Sponsor:** Submit against a currently open need. Show PENDING → runtime → Sponsor Fit → exact-offer notification → human decision. Approval contributes quantity; rejection does not.
7. **History:** Show My Sponsorships as owner and another authenticated viewer. Only approved public contributions appear on map/highlights; private offers are absent from the non-owner response.
8. **Evidence:** Show a correlated run/trace and explain validation and human authority.
