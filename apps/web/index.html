How It Works

From community goal
to coordinated action.

Hatcommways turns community goals into structured stages, roles, support needs, execution updates, and approved changes — so everyone can move from ideas to real impact, together.

Step 01

Discover or create an event

Find a community event or start with a goal of your own. Bring the purpose, place, and people together in one event.

Step 02

Define stages and roles

Break the goal into stages and work items. Define the roles and responsibilities needed to turn the plan into action.

Step 03

Join, watch, or support

Take part in meaningful work or offer resources and support. Organizers review participation and sponsor offers in the context of the event.

Step 04

Coordinate and execute

Keep everyone aligned as the work happens. Members share updates, track progress, and request changes — all in one place, with transparency and accountability.

Step 05

Approve changes and learn

Review proposed changes before they become part of the plan. Keep important decisions with the organizer and use progress and updates to guide what comes next.

Features

Human-guided
coordination,
not blind automation.

Agents help propose and coordinate, while organizers stay in control of important decisions.

  • Plan stages and work clearly

    Turn goals into actionable stages, tasks, and timelines.

  • Match people to meaningful roles

    Find the right people for the right work, based on skills and availability.

  • Track blockers and updates

    Surface risks early and keep everyone informed.

  • Review sponsor offers in context

    See offers, commitments, and fit — all in one place.

  • Selective replanning for affected work only

    When things change, agents suggest focused updates instead of overhauling everything.
